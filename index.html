How to read your air dashboard

US AQI describes outdoor pollution on a 0–500 scale. A higher number indicates greater risk. At 151 or above, the air is unhealthy for everyone. Follow local alerts when making outdoor plans. AirNow AQI guide

PM2.5 is the concentration of fine particles in micrograms per cubic meter (µg/m³). The outdoor readings are modeled estimates from Copernicus CAMS via Open-Meteo. Air quality data

Trip impact is estimated tailpipe CO₂ avoided when an average gasoline-car trip is skipped. EPA emissions estimate

Indoor air can be improved by reducing pollution at its source and filtering particles. EPA home air cleaner guide
